A story of two cats

Two cats. One home. Endless favourites.

Welcome to the world of Marsik and Sandra — two cats with unique personalities, favourite things, and plenty of opinions. Discover their story and explore the toys, accessories, and cosy essentials they love and recommend.

Our story

Marsik and Sandra came into our lives from different places, but both of them share the same beginning — they were once waiting for a home.

Now they share one home, two very different personalities, and countless little everyday moments. Marsik is calm and thoughtful, while Sandra is independent and full of character. They may not always agree on everything, but somehow they have become a little family of their own.

This website is a small collection of their story, their favourite things, and the moments that make our home feel like home.

Meet Sandra

Sandra is younger, energetic, and always ready to run, jump, and explore. She can move incredibly fast and jump surprisingly high, although she usually prefers playing on her own.

She is an independent and rather selective girl — both when it comes to food and people. But whenever we come home, she always greets us with her tail held high, as if she has been waiting for us all day.

Meet Marsik

Marsik is our calm and funny boy who loves to sleep, eat, and occasionally steal Sandra’s food. He has a special talent for making us laugh every single day, even when he is doing absolutely nothing.

Since Sandra came into our home, Marsik has become much more active. Now he is always ready to play with her and seems to have decided that being her older brother is a full-time job.
